TARGET VISIBILITY
Consider how much of each target is hidden behind another object or cut off by the image boundary. Judge missing physical parts, not missing detail from small size or image degradation. An ordinary viewpoint showing one side of an object is not itself occlusion.

Level 1: The target is fully visible, with no meaningful part hidden or cut off.
Level 2: A small part is hidden or cut off, while nearly all of the target remains visible.
Level 3: A substantial part is hidden or cut off, while most of the target remains visible.
Level 4: Most of the target is hidden or cut off, leaving only a few parts visible.
Level 5: Nearly the entire target is hidden or cut off, leaving only a small fragment visible.

Use the image-wide median level and the fraction of all targets with most of their form hidden or cut off. In evidence, distinguish occlusion from truncation when either is present.
